# Terms of use

Short version: this is a journal, not an adviser, not a broker, and not a source
of truth about your account. Use it accordingly.

## Not financial advice

Trade Journal records your screen and stores what you type about your own
trades. It does not give trading advice, recommendations, signals or analysis of
any kind. Nothing it displays is a suggestion to enter, exit, or size a
position. Every number in it came from you.

Trading futures involves substantial risk of loss and is not suitable for
everyone. Past results — including any shown in this app — do not indicate
future results.

## The numbers are your own reporting

Win rate, expectancy, profit factor, R and P&L are computed from trades you
entered by hand. They are not read from your broker, not reconciled against any
statement, and not verified by anything. They are as accurate as your own entry
and no more.

**Your broker's statement is the authoritative record of what happened in your
account. This app is not.** Where they disagree, the broker is right.

Nothing is inferred from a recording or from a marker: a video is not a
queryable record of what you traded.

## Recordings are your responsibility

You choose what to share with the app and where the files are kept. Recording
your own screen is your decision; make sure it is a lawful one where you are,
and be aware that anything visible on that screen is captured — account
numbers, balances, other people's information in a shared space.

## No warranty

The software is provided as is, without warranty of any kind. Screen recording
depends on your operating system, your browser or the app's runtime, your disk
and your drivers. A session can fail to start, stop early, or be lost to a crash
or a full disk.

**Do not rely on it as your only record of anything.** Keep your broker
statements.
